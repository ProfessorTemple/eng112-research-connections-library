Open Thesis pilot

Try one complete research cycle

1. In the Library, write a question, save one or two sources, and add your thoughts.

2. In the Discovery Bridge panel, choose those sources and select Send selected discoveries to Thesis Studio.

3. In the Thesis Studio, review the incoming material and select Use these discoveries in my thesis. Develop and save your three-story thesis.

4. Write a new research question and select Send this question back to the Library. Accept it there if you want to continue the inquiry.

Privacy and work: Nothing is sent to a server or to your instructor by these bridge controls. Data stays in browser storage on this device and is not automatically shared between devices. Because these pages share a GitHub Pages origin, use this pilot for ordinary course notes, not confidential interviews or identifying details. Download backups of important work.

Status: Prototype awaiting hands-on Safari, VoiceOver, and full accessibility testing. The original Research as Encounter application and regular Research Connections Library are unchanged.
